## The correction

The plan said top-dress and keep the pot. That was wrong, and the photo of the base is why: a dense root mat escaping the drainage holes, and two roots running a foot across the floor.

This is the second plant in the collection to genuinely need more root space, and the only one that overturned a decision already made. Everywhere else in this collection roots at the drainage holes meant it had run out of **fresh soil, not room**. Here there is a mat rather than a few strays, so it is room.

18cm to 21cm is one step. Nothing larger, because a big volume of wet compost around the root ball is a rot risk even on a plant that wants a repot.

## Everything else stays

Mature, fenestrated adult leaves. Do not combine it with the small variegated: the mature one would hold the juvenile one in juvenile form. The metal rod it already has is fine, so no pole and no trellis.

Moderate feeder. Heavy nitrogen pushes variegated pothos greener, so do not overdo it.
