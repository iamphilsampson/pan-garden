Cut back and left to reshoot. Water sparingly while it is leafless.

**Do not feed it yet.** It is a hungry plant, but feeding a leafless stem does nothing. It goes into the feeding queue only once new shoots actually show, which is why it sits fifth rather than first.

General mix if it ever needs repotting: 3 parts coir or peat, 1 perlite, 1 orchid bark, plus granular or worm castings.
