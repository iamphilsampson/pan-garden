Second of the three trailers. Cut back and the good lengths chopped into two-bump pieces and replanted.

## Making new plants from the offcuts

Lay the cut lengths out all facing the same way and keep them that way. Chop into pieces with two bumps each, cutting 1cm past a bump at both ends. Strip any leaf off the lower bump, keep any on the upper. Bin anything soft or brown. If you lose track of which way is up, the stem thins toward the tip and the leaves get smaller that way.

Poke a hole with a pencil. Lower bump 2 to 3cm under the surface, upper bump and its leaf above. Three or four pieces per hole. **The mistake that wastes the job is burying smooth stem with no bump underneath.**

Cuttings mix: 2 parts coir, 1 perlite. No granular feed, the salts scorch cut ends. Worm castings are fine, they cannot burn.

Damp not soggy, bright light, no direct sun.
