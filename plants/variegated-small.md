## Why the new pot got cancelled

It turns out to be in 16cm rather than 13, and that flips the answer. With only five or six leaves, 16cm is already a generous pot.

Roots through the drainage holes means it has run out of **fresh soil, not room**. Moving it to 19cm would leave a large volume of wet compost around a small root ball, which is a rot risk on a plant that grows slowly because half its leaf area cannot photosynthesise.

So: same pot, fresh mix.

That killed the pot cascade too, since no plum pot is freed, which is why the Dracaena is being skipped rather than given a 13cm.

## Care

- General mix: 3 parts coir or peat, 1 perlite, 1 orchid bark, plus granular or worm castings
- Moderate feeder. Heavy nitrogen pushes it greener, which defeats the point
- Do not combine it with the big variegated. The mature plant would hold this one in juvenile form
