Feeding is the only real intervention he is getting, and it is the one that matters. Same story as the other two monsteras: not enough light, no feed since the compost ran out, aerial roots hanging in the air doing nothing.

His pole is a stub the plant grew past years ago. It is the same extendable type as OG loc's, with a white plastic socket in the top, so extension sections fix both plants in one purchase.

Soil had settled well below the rim, and a thick aerial root was looping loose across the surface. Bury that root when top-dressing. In compost it becomes a working feeder root, in air it stays corky.
