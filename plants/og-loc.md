Collapsed outward, leaves on the floor, petioles over 70cm and weak. The corner is the whole story. He gets light from one side only, which is why every petiole bolted sideways and the leaves are on the floor.

**No pole shortens the petioles he already has.** It only makes the next ones come up short and stiff. So move him first, then support.

## Anchoring matters as much as height

Extending a leaning pole just gives you a taller lean. A long bamboo cane driven down beside it, with the two tied together, is what actually holds it.

**Both of his poles have white plastic sockets in the top**, which means they are the extendable kind. Take one to the shop. If the brand can be matched, three or four extension sections are cheaper and better than new poles, and they fix Philipos at the same time. If nothing matches, a length of 25mm plastic pipe or a stout cane does the job, because you are wrapping it in moss anyway.

## Why no repot

26cm with plenty of root room. His problem is light and support, not space. Check the drainage holes when he is lifted and only reconsider if roots are pouring out.
