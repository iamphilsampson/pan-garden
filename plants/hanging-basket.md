The bald centre was the problem, and the eight rooted cuttings sitting in propagation tubes were the answer, so the two jobs solved each other.

**Roots trimmed to about 5cm before planting.** They had spiralled tightly round the inside of the tubes, and a coiled root ball keeps growing in circles rather than out into the new compost. Trimming forces new growth outward.

Watering is the only thing to stay on top of here. A hanging basket has air on every side, so it dries far faster than a pot on a shelf. Genuinely damp for the first month while the cuttings establish.

Measure the pot next time it is down for watering, so the register is complete.
