Third of the three trailers, and the only philodendron in the collection.

## Refreshing the soil while it was bare

Tip the pot sideways, ease the plant out, crumble the loose old soil off the outside and the top with your fingers. Do not wash it or pull the roots apart. Back into the same pot, fill the gaps with fresh mix, firm gently.

That is the pattern for most of this collection: **fresh soil in the same pot**, not a bigger pot.

General mix: 3 parts coir or peat, 1 perlite, 1 orchid bark, plus granular or worm castings.
