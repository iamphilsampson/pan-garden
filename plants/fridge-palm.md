Deliberately left alone. Not a job that needs doing, though you can top-dress it if you feel like it.

The only reason a 13cm was ever on the list was the pot cascade, and that cascade depended on the small variegated pothos moving up a size. It measured 16cm and stayed put, so nothing was freed. Buying a pot purely to complete a cascade would be buying a pot for its own sake.

Dracaena are slow, they like being snug, and they rot if overpotted. Snug is the right answer.

## If it ever is repotted

General mix, but use **coir rather than peat**. Coir rewets better, which matters on anything rot-prone.

## Two things it does care about

- **Water.** It browns at the tips from tap water. Filtered or rainwater if that ever becomes convenient
- **Feed at half strength, occasionally.** Salt-sensitive, so it sits near the bottom of the feeding order
