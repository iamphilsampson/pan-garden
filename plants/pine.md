## The correction

Treating it as a bonsai was wrong twice over. It is a one to two year old pine seedling, so there is nothing to style yet, and the earlier two-up suggestion assumed an indoor plant. Pines need full sun and a proper winter cold period outdoors. Indoors it dies whatever you do.

**Do not feed it.** Soft late growth will not harden off before the frosts.
