One of the three trailers, all cut back on the same day. Moderate feeder, goes in the queue once it has regrown a little, so the feed fuels the regrowth rather than sitting unused.

## How the cut back was done

Every few centimetres along a stem there is a slight bump or joint where a leaf stalk joins, or a scar where one used to be. **That bump is the only place roots or shoots ever come from.** The smooth stretch between bumps grows nothing.

So: find where a stem leaves the soil, follow it out, count bumps, keep three or four, cut on the smooth part about 1cm past the last one kept. Roughly 10 to 15cm remains and the new shoots come from the bumps left behind.

Three weeks after replanting, tug gently. Resistance means roots.
