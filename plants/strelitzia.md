## The correction

The original advice treated it as a banana and told him to water it more. Wrong. Strelitzia want **less** water than a banana and are happy pot-bound, which is also why the 24cm pot stays.

## Open thread

Whether the fans still splay now it has moved. If they do, link stakes. If they do not, that purchase stays off the list.

Top-up mix if ever needed: 3 parts coir or peat, 1 perlite, a handful of bark. Coir rather than peat, it rewets better and this plant is rot-prone.
